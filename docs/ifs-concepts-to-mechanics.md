# IFS Concepts to Game Mechanics

Draft mapping for the RPG. Not final.

## Core Loop
1. Encounter a part (or system of parts) in distress or conflict.
2. Practice curiosity and non-judgment (Self energy) instead of fighting or ignoring it.
3. Listen to its positive intent and fears.
4. Negotiate or unburden (with consent of the part).
5. Integrate updated role into the internal system.

## Player Stats / Resources
- **Self Energy** (or 8 Cs meters): Builds through mindful choices; depletes when blended with extreme parts.
- **Part Trust**: Relationship scores with individual parts or groups.
- **System Harmony**: Overall balance metric.

## Example Encounters
- A Manager that over-plans every quest to avoid failure (fear of an Exile's shame).
- A Firefighter that "logs out" or starts random fights when emotional dialogue gets too close.
- An Exile locked in a memory dungeon that needs witnessing, not rescue-by-force.

## Design Principles
- Non-pathologizing: No "bad" parts to defeat.
- Consent and pacing: Parts can refuse; rushing backfires.
- Educational layer: Optional journal or glossary that reinforces real IFS language without being didactic.
- Accessibility: Suitable for people familiar or unfamiliar with IFS; clear metaphors.
