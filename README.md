# ifs-rpg

An RPG designed to teach and instill the concepts of **Internal Family Systems (IFS)** counseling/therapy methodology through interactive gameplay.

## Core Idea

Players explore an internal world populated by their "parts" (subpersonalities). The goal is to access and lead from the **Self** — the calm, curious, compassionate core — to unburden protectors and heal exiles, creating greater internal harmony.

This is a game concept and early prototype space, not a clinical tool or replacement for therapy.

## Key IFS Concepts Mapped to Gameplay

- **Parts**: NPCs or companions with distinct roles, memories, and agendas.
  - **Managers**: Proactive protectors that plan, control, criticize, or people-please to prevent pain.
  - **Firefighters**: Reactive protectors that distract or numb (e.g., rage, avoidance, compulsion) when pain surfaces.
  - **Exiles**: Vulnerable parts carrying trauma, shame, fear, or grief — often locked away.
- **Self**: The player's true leadership state, characterized by the 8 Cs (Calm, Curiosity, Clarity, Compassion, Confidence, Courage, Creativity, Connectedness). Accessing Self is a core mechanic.
- **Unburdening**: Quests or interactions where parts release extreme roles once they trust Self leadership.
- **No Bad Parts**: Every part has positive intent; the game focuses on relationship and role restoration rather than elimination.
